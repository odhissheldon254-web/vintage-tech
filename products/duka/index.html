Do I need my own web address?

No. You can start on a free address and move to your own .co.ke later. A domain usually costs around a thousand to fifteen hundred shillings a year.

No, on purpose. You add each seller after checking them, which is what keeps customers trusting the badge.

Can customers pay with M-PESA?

It doesn't take payments today. Orders are confirmed with you directly. M-PESA checkout can be added as a custom build.
