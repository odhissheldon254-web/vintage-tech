Inside

Everything a builder needs, on one screen.

Eight tools that usually live in eight windows, in one calm workspace, driven by the keyboard.

01

Boxes

Ubuntu and Kali, desktop or terminal, each in its own sealed container. Break one, reset it. Your laptop never notices.

>_

Shells in the browser

Real terminals in tabs, live and resizable. They only ever open inside a box, never on your main system.

◷

Save points

Save a box before something risky. Rewind in seconds: the system and your home folder come back exactly as they were.

▦

Memory guard

See what's eating your memory, on the laptop and in each box, and pause boxes in one click before things freeze.

⌘

Command palette

Ctrl+K, type "kali" or "save". Every action is a few keystrokes away.

◎

Systems & activity

Every project you build, with its branch, changes and last commit, plus a timeline of everything you shipped.

✎

Journal

A daily recap written from your commits, and private notes for each project. Saved on your machine only.

⬡

Desktops in windows

Full Ubuntu or Kali desktops as draggable windows inside the workspace, or popped out to a tab.

Why it exists

Built for how we really work.

Most tools assume a powerful machine and unlimited internet. Vintage Air assumes an ordinary laptop and a data bundle.
