Questions

The honest answers.

How much does it cost?

Every price is a quote, because every business is different. You get a clear number, and what it includes, before any work starts.

How do I pay?

We agree the price and the payment steps together before work begins. M-PESA is fine.

Who owns the site and the data?

You do. Your address, your content and your customers' data are yours. We set things up in your name wherever we can.

How long does it take?

A Ready product is mostly setup and branding, so it's quick. Custom work takes longer. Your quote includes a date.

What happens after launch?

Fixes and small changes continue. New features are quoted separately, so there are no surprise bills.

Is my customers' data safe?

Data is locked to your account, and we follow Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019. We never sell or share it.
